## About

I work on the definition side of software products: translating stakeholder needs into user stories with acceptance criteria, prioritising a backlog by business value, and following implementation through to delivery. I also build them, which means requirements, estimates and technical trade-offs get discussed in the same language.

My background is in Psychology and in multiplatform application development. In practice, discovery work — structured interviews, requirement elicitation, measurement design — and technical work — REST APIs, hexagonal architecture, relational and document databases — belong to the same job.

## What I'm working on

### Product Owner Junior & Dev — Worldpats (A Coruña)

Backlog ownership for a development team working in Scrum: stakeholder needs turned into user stories with acceptance criteria, backlog refinement, sprint planning and tracking in Jira. I also develop and integrate end-to-end features, with the documentation and testing that go with them.

### Founder & Product Owner — Corata · [corata.es](https://corata.es)

Corata is a talent assessment product that builds a professional profile from observed behaviour instead of a static CV: candidates work through real scenarios, and the profile reflects how they prioritise, structure a decision and adapt under pressure.

I define the vision and the roadmap, and prioritise the backlog by business value. Discovery so far has been built on interviews with decision makers at five organisations — staffing agencies, SMEs, startups and education centres. I built the MVP as a full stack developer (REST API, API-first approach); development is now handled by a technical partner so that my focus stays on product and business: market and competitor analysis, pitch, and relationships across the startup ecosystem.

> Corata won the regional and the Galicia-wide stages of the Santander X programme (Banco Santander) and is currently a candidate for the national award.
